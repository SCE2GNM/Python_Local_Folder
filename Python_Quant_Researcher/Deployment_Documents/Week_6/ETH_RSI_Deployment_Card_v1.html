Strategy Overview

What it does

A mean reversion strategy — profits from ETH recovering after short-term oversold extremes, not from riding trends. Mean reversion strategy on ETH/USDT daily candles. Buys when ETH is oversold (RSI below 43) while in a bull regime (price above the 120-day SMA). Waits for momentum to recover, then exits when RSI rises back to 48. A hard stop at 15% below entry protects against sustained downtrends breaking through the regime filter.

Entry condition

RSI(14) < 43 AND Close > 120-day SMA. RSI uses Wilder's exponential smoothing (alpha = 1/14). Both conditions must hold at the daily close. The SMA filter is the key regime gate — it prevents buying dips in bear markets where dips continue falling.

Exit conditions

RSI recovery: exit at close when RSI > 48. The 5-point gap between entry (43) and exit (48) gives the trade room to recover before triggering exit, reducing premature exits from single-bar noise.
Hard stop: 15% below entry price, checked against daily LOW. Stop is fixed at entry — does not trail. If daily LOW ≤ stop price, exits at stop price (gap protection).

Why it works

ETH in a confirmed bull regime (above 120 SMA) reliably recovers from short oversold readings. The 120 SMA filter eliminates the high-loss environment of sustained bear markets. Result: 93.5% backtest win rate across 31 trades, profit factor 5.395. The strategy sits flat most of the time — flat periods are capital preservation, not dead weight.

Risk Register — Open Items

RR-RSI-002 High 🚨 MOST URGENT Stop order monitoring absent

The bot does not verify that its Binance stop order is still ACTIVE on each scheduled run. Binance silently cancels resting stop orders under certain conditions (maintenance, order age, account flags). If the stop is cancelled without Telegram alert, the position is unprotected. This has already occurred in ETH ADX live trading — emergency manual intervention was required.

Current mitigation: $150 capital cap limits maximum unprotected exposure. At 15% stop distance on $341 position, maximum stop-loss = $51. In an extreme move (−50%), unprotected loss = $170 — limited at $150 cap.
Required fix: On every bot run while LONG: call get_order(orderId=state['stop_loss_order_id']). If status ≠ NEW, re-place immediately and send Telegram alert.

⛔ Blocker: must be resolved before any capital increase beyond $150
RR-RSI-001 High Win rate sensitivity — negative Kelly below 72.2%

The strategy has negative Kelly expectation at win rates below 72.2%. The backtest win rate (93.5%) is almost certainly inflated relative to live performance. Mean reversion strategies are vulnerable to regime shifts — a sustained downtrend produces consecutive stop-losses that rapidly degrade the win rate. At 75% live win rate (moderate scenario), median annual return is −0.1% with 55.5% probability of a negative year.

Mitigation: Position size set at $341 (Kelly-optimal at 75% WR conservative scenario). Running win rate monitored after every trade. Trading paused if running WR falls below 72% over 20+ trades. Monte Carlo analysis fully documented in this card (see performance section).

RR-RSI-003 High Position sizing: full Kelly at backtest rate would require 5× leverage

At the backtest win rate (93.5%), full Kelly position = $2,555 — requiring 5.1× leverage, which is unavailable on Binance Spot and inappropriate for an unleveraged strategy. At 75% live win rate, Kelly-optimal position is $341, which is below the $495 unleveraged cap. Deploying at $495 with 75% live WR would mean over-sizing relative to Kelly — accelerating capital erosion if win rate degrades.

Mitigation: Initial deployment at $341 (Kelly at 75% WR). Scale to $495 only after 20 live trades confirm running WR ≥ 80%. Kelly fraction recalculated after each block of 10 live trades.

RR-RSI-004 Major Sample size: 31 trades — wide confidence intervals on all metrics

31 backtest trades over 6.5 years. At 93.5% observed win rate, the 95% confidence interval on win rate spans approximately 79%–99% (Wilson interval). The lower bound (79%) is already a scenario where 25% of live years are negative. A single additional loss from a 32nd trade changes win rate from 93.5% to 90.6%.

Mitigation: Monte Carlo confidence intervals used as deployment basis rather than point estimates. Do not adjust position sizing based on live results until minimum 20 live trades accumulated. Flag in deployment document that 95% CI on win rate spans 79%–99%.

RR-RSI-005 Major 120 SMA regime filter: data-mining risk on 2018–2026 data

The 120-day SMA filter was selected during optimisation on the full 2018–2026 backtest window. It has not been independently validated on out-of-sample data. The filter may be inadvertently calibrated to the specific bear market characteristics of 2018–2019 and 2022, rather than identifying a structural signal that will persist into future regimes.

Mitigation: Risk acknowledged in deployment document. 120 SMA filter status displayed in daily health check Telegram message. Monitor closely during any sustained price decline below 120 SMA — strategy should sit in cash during that period. Full re-evaluation after 50 live trades.

RR-RSI-006 Major Formal stability classification not completed

STABLE / MARGINAL / FRAGILE classification has not been run with formal methodology. Informal per-parameter stability scores (RSI period 80%, oversold 67%, exit level 100%, stop 80%, MA filter 100% above PF 2.0) suggest STABLE, but this has not been confirmed with a grid-based composite score. A FRAGILE result would indicate potential overfitting.

Mitigation: Informal stability scan results are encouraging and consistent with a broad parameter plateau. Formal classification targeted for Week 7. Result will inform confidence score in capital allocation framework (SI007).
